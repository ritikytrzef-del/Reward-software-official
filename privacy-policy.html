Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

This page explains what information is collected when you use any of the Telegram earning bots listed on Reward Ledger, and how that information is used.

1. Information we collect

When you open and use a listed bot, we may collect:

  • Your Telegram user ID, username, and profile name, as shared with us by Telegram.
  • Activity within the bot, such as ads watched, coins earned, tasks completed, and daily bonus claims.
  • Withdrawal details you submit, including your FaucetPay email, CWallet ID, or Binance Pay / Binance Red Packet identifier, solely for processing your payout.
  • Basic technical data such as device type and app version, used for troubleshooting.

2. How we use your information

  • To operate your account, track your coin balance, and calculate rewards.
  • To process withdrawal requests through FaucetPay, CWallet, Binance Red Packet, or Binance Pay.
  • To detect fraud, multi-accounting, or abuse of the ad-reward system.
  • To send you bot notifications about withdrawals, bonuses, or account status.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information to third parties.
  • We do not request or store your wallet private keys or exchange passwords.
  • We do not access your Telegram messages outside the bot conversation itself.

4. Third-party services

Bots listed here may integrate ad networks and offerwall partners to display rewarded ads, and payout processors (FaucetPay, CWallet, Binance) to complete withdrawals. These third parties operate under their own privacy policies, and some data — such as your withdrawal identifier — is necessarily shared with them to complete a payout.

5. Data retention

Account and transaction data is retained for as long as your bot account is active, and for a reasonable period afterward to resolve disputes, prevent fraud, or comply with payout processor requirements.

6. Your choices

You can stop using any bot at any time by blocking it in Telegram. You may contact the bot admin through the in-app support option to request account or data deletion, subject to any pending withdrawal or fraud checks.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of a listed bot after changes are posted means you accept the revised policy.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, reach out through the support option inside any of the listed bots.
